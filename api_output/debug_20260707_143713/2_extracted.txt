### Post Name(s)
Agniveervayu (Intake 02/2027)

### Vacancy Details
The document does not specify the total number of vacancies. Vacancies are allocated state-wise and will be used for the preparation of the Provisional Select List (PSL).

### Pay Scale
Agniveervayu will be paid a monthly package with a fixed yearly increment:
*   **1st Year:** ₹30,000 per month
*   **2nd Year:** ₹33,000 per month
*   **3rd Year:** ₹36,500 per month
*   **4th Year:** ₹40,000 per month
*   **Terminal Benefit:** 'Seva Nidhi' package of approximately ₹10.04 Lakhs (including interest) upon completion of 4 years.

### Educational Qualification
*   **Science Subjects:** Passed Intermediate/10+2/Equivalent with Mathematics, Physics, and English with minimum 50% marks in aggregate and 50% in English; **OR** Passed 3-year Diploma in Engineering (Mechanical/Electrical/Electronics/Automobile/Computer Science/Instrumentation Technology/IT) with 50% marks in aggregate and 50% in English; **OR** Passed 2-year Vocational Course with non-vocational subjects (Physics and Mathematics) with 50% marks in aggregate and 50% in English.
*   **Other than Science Subjects:** Passed Intermediate/10+2/Equivalent in any stream with minimum 50% marks in aggregate and 50% in English; **OR** Passed 2-year Vocational Course with minimum 50% marks in aggregate and 50% in English.

### Age Limit
*   Candidates must be born between 01 July 2005 and 01 January 2010 (both dates inclusive).
*   Upper age limit as on date of enrolment should be 22 years.

### Application Fee
₹550/- plus 18% GST (Non-refundable).

### Selection Process
1.  **Phase-I:** Online Test (Objective Type).
2.  **Phase-II:** Physical Fitness Test (PFT), Adaptability Test-I, and Adaptability Test-II.
3.  **Phase-III:** Medical Examination.

### Exam Pattern
*   **Science Subjects:** 60 minutes duration; comprises Physics, Mathematics, and English (10+2 CBSE syllabus).
*   **Other than Science Subjects:** 45 minutes duration; comprises English (10+2 CBSE syllabus) and Reasoning & General Awareness (RAGA).
*   **Marking:** +1 for correct answer, 0 for unattempted, -0.25 for each wrong answer.
