### Pay Scale
Agniveer package of ₹30,000/- per month with a fixed yearly increment.
*   **1st Year:** ₹30,000/- per month
*   **2nd Year:** ₹33,000/- per month
*   **3rd Year:** ₹36,500/- per month
*   **4th Year:** ₹40,000/- per month
(Plus applicable Risk and Hardship allowances, Dress and Travel allowances).

### Educational Qualification
*   **Science Subjects:** Intermediate/10+2/Equivalent with Mathematics, Physics, and English with minimum 50% marks in aggregate and 50% marks in English. **OR** Three years Diploma Course in Engineering (Mechanical/Electrical/Electronics/Automobile/Computer Science/Instrumentation Technology/Information Technology) with 50% marks in aggregate and 50% marks in English. **OR** Two years Vocational Course with non-vocational subjects (Physics and Mathematics) with 50% marks in aggregate and 50% marks in English.
*   **Other than Science Subjects:** Intermediate/10+2/Equivalent in any stream/subjects with minimum 50% marks in aggregate and 50% marks in English. **OR** Two years Vocational Course with minimum 50% marks in aggregate and 50% marks in English.

### Age Limit
Candidates born between 01 July 2005 and 01 January 2010 (both dates inclusive). The upper age limit as on the date of enrolment should be 22 years.

### Application Fee
₹550/- plus 18% GST (Non-refundable).

### Selection Process
1.  **Phase-I:** Online Test (Objective Type).
2.  **Phase-II:** Physical Fitness Test (PFT), Adaptability Test-I, and Adaptability Test-II.
3.  **Phase-III:** Medical Examination.

### Exam Pattern
*   **Science Subjects:** 60 minutes duration; comprises Physics, Mathematics, and English (10+2 CBSE syllabus).
*   **Other than Science Subjects:** 45 minutes duration; comprises English (10+2 CBSE syllabus) and Reasoning & General Awareness (RAGA).
*   **Marking:** 1 mark for every correct answer, 0 for unattempted, and 0.25 deduction for each wrong answer.
