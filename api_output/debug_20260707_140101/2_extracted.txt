### Post Name(s)
Agniveervayu (Intake 02/2027)

### Vacancy Details
The document does not specify the total number of vacancies. Vacancies are allocated state-wise and will be used for the preparation of the Provisional Select List (PSL).

### Pay Scale
*   **1st Year:** ₹30,000/- per month
*   **2nd Year:** ₹33,000/- per month
*   **3rd Year:** ₹36,500/- per month
*   **4th Year:** ₹40,000/- per month
*   *Additional benefits:* Risk and Hardship allowances, Dress and Travel allowances, Ration, Clothing, Accommodation, and Leave Travel Concession (LTC).
*   *Terminal Benefit:* 'Seva Nidhi' package of approximately ₹10.04 Lakhs (including interest) upon completion of 4 years.

### Educational Qualification
*   **Science Subjects:** Passed Intermediate/10+2/Equivalent with Mathematics, Physics, and English with minimum 50% marks in aggregate and 50% marks in English; **OR** Passed 3-year Diploma in Engineering (Mechanical/Electrical/Electronics/Automobile/Computer Science/Instrumentation Technology/IT) with 50% marks in aggregate and 50% in English; **OR** Passed 2-year Vocational Course with non-vocational subjects (Physics and Mathematics) with 50% marks in aggregate and 50% in English.
*   **Other than Science Subjects:** Passed Intermediate/10+2/Equivalent in any stream with minimum 50% marks in aggregate and 50% marks in English; **OR** Passed 2-year Vocational Course with minimum 50% marks in aggregate and 50% in English.

### Age Limit
*   Candidates born between 01 July 2005 and 01 January 2010 (both dates inclusive).
*   Upper age limit as on date of enrolment should be 22 years.

### Application Fee
₹550/- plus 18% GST (Non-refundable).

### Selection Process
1.  **Phase-I:** Online Test (Objective Type).
2.  **Phase-II:** Physical Fitness Test (PFT), Adaptability Test-I, and Adaptability Test-II.
3.  **Phase-III:** Medical Examination.

### Exam Pattern
*   **Science Subjects:** 60 minutes duration; comprises Physics, Mathematics, and English (10+2 CBSE syllabus).
*   **Other than Science Subjects:** 45 minutes duration; comprises English (10+2 CBSE syllabus) and Reasoning & General Awareness (RAGA).
*   **Marking:** +1 for correct answer, 0 for unattempted, -0.25 for each wrong answer.

### Important Dates
*   **Online Registration:** 06 July 2026 to 26 July 2026
*   **Online Exam:** 22-23 September 2026
*   **Provisional Select List (PSL) Display:** Last week of May 2027
*   **Enrolment List Publication:** After 01 June 2027

### Important Notes
*   Only unmarried Indian male and female candidates are eligible.
*   Candidates must not marry during the 4-year engagement period.
*   Permanent body tattoos are generally not permitted (exceptions for inner forearms/tribal customs).
*   Candidates are not entitled to Ex-Servicemen (ESM) status after discharge.
*   No TA/DA is admissible for any stage of the selection process.
*   Candidates must carry a valid AADHAAR card (exemptions for J&K, Ladakh, Assam, and Meghalaya).
